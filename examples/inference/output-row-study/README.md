# Output-row study — historical held candidate

These are diagnostic artifacts from 2026-11-03 on the Pixel Debian VM, before the completion-wait repair. This original candidate was **not adopted**. Its results do not establish a decode speedup or a larger supported model. The [later readback study](../readback-study/) preserves both further failures and corrected fixed-prompt results. See [the study and recovery notes](../../../docs/large-output.md).

- `baseline-scheduler.log` / `candidate-scheduler.log` are raw logs; `*-placement.json` parses their final graph. Placement is not model correctness or a timing comparison.
- `large-final-{1,2}.jsonl` passed a separate-weight-buffer Q8_0 basis-vector test. The earlier `large-after.jsonl` and `large-barrier.jsonl` failed; their logs are retained. The earlier dense-input `small.jsonl` used a different CPU quantized-activation reference, so its strict per-element failures cannot be silently relabelled as passes.
- `logits-{0,1}.*` use the partially rebuilt runtime; `matched-{0,1}.*` use tools/core/CPU built from one source. `0` disables row splitting; `1` enables it. Each `.bin` contains 151936 little-endian float32 values. **Both split vectors are entirely zero.** The flag-off vectors are diagnostic GPU-decoder runs, not qualified CPU references, and their values changed between runs.
- The old dumper's `status: pass` checked finite values and completion only. It erroneously accepted zero vectors. Original receipts are preserved. `summary.json` reports the independently counted zero values and held status. The new `check-model` rejects this case.
- `callback-1.json` is intentionally empty: the callback probe never completed. Its log and the events already captured are retained. A black GUI and Android watchdog traces were observed; the cause is unresolved. Normal ADB reboot restored the device, and the collaborator confirmed the lock screen and unlock. GPU experiments stopped at that point; the later study restarted with small transfer checks.
- `runtime-sha256.json` inventories the preserved study runtimes after recovery. `source-sha256.json` covers the five modified source files: they matched a fresh pinned source with the original nine-patch stack applied, before `completion-wait`. `sha256.json` hashes the exported files. This is a historical snapshot, not a claim that a later build reproduces identical binaries.

The later transfer-only reproducer exposed stale and zero reads. The opt-in completion-wait repair subsequently passed the new readback, kernel, and fixed-prompt full-model gates on the recovered Pixel. Those new results do not relabel the zero outputs in this directory. Dense general large-matrix behavior, broad model quality, and maximum capacity remain unqualified.
